Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

In short

We collect the details you put on your card and the information needed to run your account. We use it to provide the service, we don't sell it, and you can ask us to delete it.

On this page

    Who we are

    Virtual Business Card India ("VB India", "we", "us") provides digital business cards and NFC smart cards from Ahmedabad, Gujarat. This policy explains what information we collect when you use vbcardindia.com and our cards, and how we use it.

    Information we collect

    We collect information you give us and some information collected automatically:

    • Account details: company name, contact person, email address, mobile number and password.
    • Card content: anything you add to your card, such as photos, products, videos, brochures, address, GST number and payment details like a UPI ID or bank account.
    • Enquiries and reviews: messages and ratings your visitors submit through your card.
    • Usage data: pages viewed, device and browser type, approximate location and the date and time of visits, used to keep the service working and to show you card statistics.

    How we use your information

    • To create and host your digital card and keep it online
    • To process payments and send invoices
    • To send you service messages, such as login codes and renewal reminders
    • To answer your support requests
    • To improve our product and prevent misuse

    We only send marketing messages if you agree to them, and you can opt out at any time.

    What is public on your card

    Your card is designed to be shared. Anything you publish on it, including your name, phone number, email, address and payment details, can be seen by anyone who has the link, scans the QR code or taps the NFC card. Only add information you are comfortable sharing publicly.

    Sharing your information

    We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

    • Service providers who help us run the service, such as hosting, SMS, email and payment companies, under confidentiality obligations
    • Authorities, when the law requires us to

    Cookies

    We use a small number of cookies and similar storage to keep you logged in, remember your theme choice and understand how the site is used. You can clear or block cookies in your browser, but some parts of the site may not work without them.

    How long we keep data

    We keep your account information while your account is active. If you close your account, we delete or anonymise your card content within a reasonable period, except where we must keep records such as invoices for legal or tax reasons.

    Your choices and rights

    You can view and edit your card details at any time from your dashboard. You can also ask us to send you a copy of your information, correct it or delete your account by contacting us using the details below.

    Security

    We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections. No online service can be completely secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

    Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will tell you by email or through your dashboard. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
