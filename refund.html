Refund policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

In short

Try the service free before you pay. Plan refunds depend on when you ask, and printed NFC cards can't be refunded once production starts, unless they arrive faulty.

On this page

    Free trial first

    We offer a free trial so you can build and share a full card before paying. We recommend using the trial to make sure the service suits you.

    Subscription plans

    • If you cancel within 7 days of your first payment and contact us, we will refund that payment in full
    • After 7 days, payments are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time and your card stays online until the end of the period you paid for
    • Renewal payments are not refunded once the new period has started; cancel before the renewal date to avoid being charged

    NFC and printed cards

    • Orders can be cancelled for a full refund until you approve the final artwork
    • Once printing has started, orders can't be cancelled or refunded
    • If a card arrives damaged or the NFC chip doesn't work, tell us within 7 days of delivery and we'll replace it free of charge

    How to request a refund

    Email support@vbcardindia.com or WhatsApp us from your registered mobile number with your company name, payment date and the reason for the request. For faulty NFC cards, please include a photo or short video.

    When you'll receive it

    Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method. Once we've processed it, it usually takes 5 to 10 working days to appear, depending on your bank.

    Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy from time to time. Changes apply to purchases made after the update.
